# Survey Creative & Privacy Governance Skill

This skill governs the strategic, creative, and regulatory dimensions of the VoiceVerification AI platform.

## 1. Creative Survey Design Principles (Voice-First B2B)
- **Engineers' Cognitive Load:** Keep questions action-oriented, concrete, and rooted in operational reality (e.g. OEE, CIP chemicals, lead time, downtime cost, EU 1935/2004 compliance).
- **Voice Encouragement & Micro-Copy:** Reassure the participant that raw, conversational speech is preferred over polished corporate speech.
- **Progressive Depth:** 3 core questions (max ~3 mins) with contextual AI follow-up queries.
- **Give-to-Get Incentive:** Offer valuable industry intelligence (e.g. aggregated peer benchmark report) in exchange for their voice insights.

## 2. Privacy, KVKK & GDPR Legal Compliance Standards
- **Strict Non-Biometric Boundary:** Explicitly guarantee that voice recordings are never processed for acoustic fingerprinting, biometric verification, or voice synthesis. They are strictly processed via speech-to-text NLP models for qualitative market validation.
- **Two-Tier Consent:** Mandatory checkbox for data processing + optional on-demand modal detailing rights under KVKK Art. 11 / GDPR Art. 15-22.
- **Data Retention & Erasure:** Raw audio purged within 30 days of campaign completion; anonymized transcript retained for aggregate research reporting.
- **Terms & Conditions:** Protection of proprietary operational trade secrets, pre-commercial research disclaimers, and limitation of liability.
